About the Founder

Experience, artistry, and personalized beauty care

Pujan Beauty Studio was founded by Pujan Pokhrel, a beauty professional with more than 10 years of experience in the cosmetics and beauty industry.

Throughout her career, Pujan has worked with internationally recognized beauty brands, including MAC Cosmetics and Sephora. Her experience has helped her develop strong expertise in professional makeup application, skincare, product selection, color matching, and creating personalized looks for different occasions.

Pujan has also completed her cosmetology certification through the North Carolina Board of Cosmetic Art Examiners. She combines professional training, industry experience, and personal attention to help every client feel confident, comfortable, and beautifully prepared.
